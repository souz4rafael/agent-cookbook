Fidelity guide

Kept: the distinction between first-contact advice and a handoff, impact versus urgency, a single focused question, and a human inspecting the summary. The official Gallery page is the recipe source link, not a claim of a running service.
Changed: the print room, shift board, article, desk policy, dialogue, note format, rules and expected examples are original educational fixtures. Impact and urgency are separate supplied observations rather than inferred P1-P4 ratings. Unknown is preserved; one known high-impact or time-critical signal is enough to offer review. There is no operational severity assignment.
Implemented here: local Studio question rules retrieve selected fictional text; the separate editable lab uses ordered first-match rules. A missing required document withholds the result. A disabled ticket tool leaves an unsubmitted, readable note. Asking for advice only does not even prepare that note. The host's confirmation dialog is the only writer of a local simulated ticket; Cancel writes nothing. Guided conversations and case evaluation never create tickets.
Not adapted: credentials or recovery procedures, device identifiers, logs, network playbooks, live knowledge search, external ITSM dispatch, Teams or other channels, delivery notifications and automatic text redaction. Source declarations and the visual canvas are reference only. The local shelf and review queue do not bind any of them.
Review check: run the whole-shift case, inspect the four observations and cancel in the host. Check that the ticket list has not changed. Repeat, confirm the reviewed slip, and check for exactly one new local record. The lab's handoff-ready expectation tests an offer, not a completed action; the journey's action-confirmed step checks the latter.

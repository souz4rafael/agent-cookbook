Foldline shelf note | When Pinned looks empty

Use this article when one desk can wait until later, Pinned shows no cards, and the view check has not been tried. Pinned is a personal display of chosen cards, not the full shift list. An empty view does not tell us whether a card was lost.

In the fictional Foldline board, switch from Pinned to All cards and look for the current shift card. Does it appear there? This changes only what the reader sees.

If the card appears, read it there; no ticket is needed for that observation. If the view still looks empty, record "no change" and offer human review rather than repeating the check. Never mark a problem resolved simply because an article was shown.

This article does not apply to a blank card page. It does not ask anyone to edit a board, change permissions, inspect a device or test a network.

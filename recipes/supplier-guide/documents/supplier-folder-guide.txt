Fictional training material. Not an official service record or policy.

FG-6 is the public reading guide, not a list of suppliers. A supplier may see only its own matched folder in this exercise.
FG-6 §2: read the edition marked signed and the last register marker together. An undated or superseded sheet cannot supply a deadline.
FG-6 §4: the Repairs Desk accepts a Correction Slip quoting the schedule line, revision and entry being replaced. Keep the original receipt. Lodging a slip does not move the signed date.

Find the signed edition first. Use the last register marker to choose a line, then read the item and its due date from that line.

A blank marker is a reason to ask the receiving desk for the register, not to guess that the first item is late.

Keep the original receipt. Send the Repairs Desk a Correction Slip naming the schedule line, its revision and the entry being replaced.

Keep the existing due date unless a signed replacement schedule supplies another. A correction receipt is not an extension.

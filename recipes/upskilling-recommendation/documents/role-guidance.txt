EXAMPLE COUNCIL - FICTIONAL LEARNING PATHWAYS
Learning edition 2. Not recruitment criteria or official careers advice.

This fictional catalogue covers two learning pathways: service administration, which practises clear communication, reliable records and responsible information handling; and junior reporting, which practises spreadsheet use, data quality and explaining charts. These are illustrative learning goals, not recruitment requirements.

Map only the experience the person chooses to describe. A skill not mentioned is unknown, not a weakness. Ask about the person's preferred tasks and learning goal before suggesting a pathway. Do not rank applicants, infer protected characteristics or decide who should be hired.

For this limited interactive mapping, service administration tracks reliable record keeping and responsible information handling. Junior reporting tracks data quality checking and chart explanation. Communication and spreadsheet use provide broader context; the four checkboxes are not a complete assessment of either role.

Choose the goal explicitly and mark only self-reported recorded skills. Suggest resources for the chosen role's unrecorded skills, in catalogue order. Do not prescribe a gap course when both tracked skills for that role are already recorded; suggest optional practice instead. Recorded skills are not verified competence or certification.

An unclear goal must not produce an invented playlist. Offer an optional adviser review using the stated interests, goal and recorded skills. Ask the person to correct the draft and confirm separately before any local request. Both this guidance and the course catalogue are prerequisites.

LEARN-101, LEARN-102 and LEARN-103 are raw-input examples, not required knowledge or fixed answers. The current lab values determine the playlist or adviser draft. Nothing is inferred from a real CV.

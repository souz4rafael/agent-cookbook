Alderfen | Park Sign Location Note: two editions
Fictional training material. Not an official service record or policy.

This fictional learning notice covers routine reports of faded park signs. Two signs may stand on opposite sides of the same path bay.

Through 11 June 2032 the public exercise requires a bay code only. A report received before the change stays on the earlier routine, even if a planner reads it later. The revised routine applies to reports received on or after 12 June 2032. Bay and side together identify which sign is intended. Do not apply the revision retrospectively or choose an edition from a processing date. If receipt date is unknown, clarify it before choosing.

Under the revision, intake asks for canal side or garden side if the side is missing and keeps the card open. If supplied, intake records the side alongside the bay code. Planning does not guess: return an incomplete card to intake on the same reference. When both labels are present, the planner can queue the exercise card. This public training note has no authority to update a live policy or service queue.

Use the date the report was received. The revised routine starts with reports received on 12 June 2032, not reports first reviewed that day.

For a report received on 11 June, the earlier bay-only routine remains in use even when someone reads it after the change.

Intake asks which side of the bay is meant. Planning returns an incomplete card to intake on the same reference rather than guessing or opening a duplicate.

For a side already supplied, intake records it with the bay code and planning uses both labels in the practice queue.

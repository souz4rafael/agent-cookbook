Alderfen | Audit reading protocol · AR-11
Fictional training material. Not an official service record or policy.

AR-11 separates three questions: what was declared, what was posted, and what basis was used. It contains no taxpayer rows.
§1: require the audit assignment and a common case key. Verify the seasonal period on each source; a posting date alone is not a period.
§2: identify a posted journal entry and a signed basis note. Leave pending, absent and recorded zero distinct. Check like units before comparing.
§3: record equal or different amounts, not a liability. Refer unresolved evidence to the auditor without inferring motive or imposing a charge.

Use the declaration period and the allocation period on the payment row. Read the period on the justification too; the day money was entered is not enough.

If the periods differ, request a like-period source rather than offsetting one season against another.

Mark it pending and leave reconciliation open. A pending amount is not a posted amount; a missing row is not a posted zero.

Look for a confirmed posting tied to the same case and period before comparing it with the declaration.

State the declared amount, the posted amount and what the justification says about their basis. Ask the auditor to resolve the difference.

A numerical mismatch alone establishes neither wrongdoing nor an assessed amount.

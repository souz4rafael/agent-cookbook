Reading note for the facsimile:
CN-12.pdf, page 2, Return allowance shows USD 54.50 in the buyer's favour. Its signed effect on PQ-48 is USD -54.50, not another charge.
The ten boards at USD 5.45 explain the printed allowance; the line calculation and its total are the same credit, not two credits.

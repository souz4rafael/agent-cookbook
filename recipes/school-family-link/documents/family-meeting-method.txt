Alderfen | Confidential meeting method
Fictional training material. Not an official service record or policy.

Family Partnership Lead | facilitation card 3.2 | 2 November 2026.
Invite the family to choose a fictional statement in their own voice or to pass. Record “not shared” rather than supply a reason. Keep the explanation separate from register marks and avoid character judgements. Only matched participants have a part in this exercise.
Read back two concrete contributions, one from each side, and a check-in date and time. Both participants must confirm all three; a staff suggestion alone is not an agreement. If either declines or withdraws, stop. A private read-back stays in the meeting: it is not correspondence or an instruction to a teacher.

Invite the family to give its own explanation or to pass. Use their words and keep the register observation separate; it does not tell you why the morning was difficult.

If a family prefers not to share, record that no explanation was shared. Do not ask for identifying details, add a label or turn silence into a reason.

Read back what the family will try, what the school will do and when both will check in. Call it agreed only if both participants confirm those same terms.

If only one side confirms, keep it as a proposal. A private read-back is not permission to send a message or share a record outside the meeting.

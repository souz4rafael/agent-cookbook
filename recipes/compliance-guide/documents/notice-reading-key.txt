Alderfen | Reading a served notice
Fictional training material. Not an official service record or policy.

Desk edition 2.1 | 20 October 2026 | Alderfen Notice Review Officer.
Use the endorsed copy actually served, including any valid amendment. Compare the service entry with the version and time limit printed on that copy. A print date, a draft deadline and a phone recollection are not substitutes. Use calendar or working days only as the served text specifies.
Copy the instruction and its location. List acceptable proof separately from proof submitted and proof accepted. If service is disputed, the copy is unavailable, or the period has elapsed, ask the Notice Review Officer to check the file. Do not set a new time, record a breach or impose a sanction.

Use the service endorsement and the counting instruction on the served copy, then check the result against its printed deadline.

If the copy is absent, service is disputed or the deadline has passed, ask the Notice Review Officer for a human review. Do not restart the clock.

Read the proof list attached to the operative clause. Use a listed format and keep the date and location needed to tie the item to the corrective step.

A receipt records delivery, not acceptance. Ask a reviewer about an alternative format rather than promising it will be sufficient.

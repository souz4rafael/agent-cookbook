Alderfen | Schoolday travel: two invitation editions
Fictional training material. Not an official service record or policy.

Compare what counts as evidence of the travel period, not who qualifies for help.

The practice scheme has two editions. An invitation issued through 30 September 2031 lists last term’s route card. An invitation issued from 1 October 2031 lists a term slip with the first and last travel dates. Delivery date, first school day and the date someone opens the envelope are not the switch. A route card alone does not show both dates for the later edition. The slip documents the period; supplying it is not an award.

For a later invitation, check that both date labels can be read. Use the return channel printed on that invitation. If a slip is unavailable, ask the issuing desk what alternative it will consider before gathering other material. Do not ask for a child's schedule in chat. For an undated or incomplete copy, ask for the invitation edition in an accessible format rather than guessing from the colour of the paper.

Use the invitation’s issue date. Before 1 October 2031 the sample lists the route card; from that date it lists a term slip showing the first and last travel dates.

If the family cannot tell which edition it has, ask the issuing desk for a dated copy rather than guessing from when the letter arrived.

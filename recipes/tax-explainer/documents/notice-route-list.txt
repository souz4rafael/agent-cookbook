NR-8 is the official route list within this fictional scheme; there is no live form or service.
P1 — Plan Desk: on an issued notice, request instalments by giving the notice reference and a proposed schedule. The desk considers the request; no plan is guaranteed.
C2 — Notice Desk: identify a wrong bay count or season and attach the relevant register extract. The desk accepts factual corrections on a working card or an issued notice.
R3 — Review Clerk: to challenge the charging basis on an issued notice, quote its reference and the disputed tariff clause with reasons. A request alone does not revise a balance.

For an issued notice, the listed instalment route is Plan Desk P1. Quote the notice reference and propose a payment schedule for the desk to consider.

Asking is not approval. The desk must review the request; the leaflet does not determine eligibility or change a balance.

For a wrong bay count or season, use Notice Desk C2 and identify the register fact to correct. To dispute the charging basis on an issued notice, give the Review Clerk your reasons through R3.

A correction can be raised on a working card. A challenge to the basis is an issued-notice route. Neither submission revises a balance on its own.

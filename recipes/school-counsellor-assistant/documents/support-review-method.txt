Pastoral Practice Lead | method 1.4 | 30 September 2026.
An alert is a prompt to compare session-level register marks with signed amendments. Quote the dates, sessions and mark meanings, recording the source version. A mark is not an explanation, a risk score or a forecast. If there is no countersigned entry, identify exactly what is missing and stop short of a complete count.
Ask what the student or family wishes to add; record a missing account as “not recorded”. Keep the review within the assigned role. Add an owner by professional role and a follow-up date only after the participants agree to both. A tentative offer, a blank consent box or a correction that removes the alert cannot create an appointment.

It points to session marks that need checking. Read the signed entries and any amendments; report the session and mark, not an assumed explanation or full-day absence.

If an entry or the student’s account is missing, say which is missing. A provisional count is not evidence of a pattern and cannot support a prediction.

Record the professional role and date only when the participants have agreed to both for this review. An offer of a meeting is not confirmation.

If either part is unconfirmed or the agreement is withdrawn, leave both fields unset. Do not book a meeting or treat the note as a direction to discipline anyone.

Alderfen | After register: alternative paper packets
Fictional training material. Not an official service record or policy.

Window two covers exercise days 11–20 inclusive. Use ONE packet with the before register, never both after packets together. Copy and Plain replay the same four standard single-sheet templates A–D. Copy tests a slip that carries the accepted edition seal forward. Plain is an alternative fictional run of the old form.

COPY PACKET
A — Day 11 Intake accepts seal; day 12 Print reads copied seal; day 14 Print releases.
B — Day 12 Intake accepts seal; day 13 Print reads copied seal; day 16 Print releases.
C — Day 13 Intake accepts seal; day 14 Print gets a blank seal box and returns the job to Intake for the edition seal; day 15 Intake copies the same seal; day 16 Print receives it; day 17 Print releases.
D — Day 14 Intake accepts seal; day 15 Print reads copied seal; day 17 Print releases.

PLAIN PACKET (alternative, not a third period)
A — Day 11 Intake accepts seal; day 12 Print returns job to Intake for the same edition seal; day 13 Intake copies it; day 14 Print receives; day 16 Print releases.
B — Day 12 Intake accepts seal; day 13 Print returns job to Intake for the same edition seal; day 15 Intake copies it; day 16 Print receives; day 18 Print releases.
C — Day 13 Intake accepts seal; day 14 Print returns job to Intake for the same edition seal; day 15 Intake copies it; day 16 Print receives; day 17 Print releases.
D — Day 14 Intake accepts seal; day 15 Print reads seal; day 17 Print releases without a return.

EXCEPTION PACKETS (each a separate variation on Copy)
Mixed stock: keep A–C. Replace D with X, an oversize foldout: day 14 Intake accepts seal, day 15 Print reads seal, day 17 Print releases. The tempting totals still cover four jobs, but X is not the same template or work type.
Two pairs: only A and B are available from each register. Do not silently bring C and D back into the denominator.
Torn slip: keep all Copy events except C’s day 17 release stamp. Day 16 is a receipt at Print, not a release. The common endpoint is unknown for C.

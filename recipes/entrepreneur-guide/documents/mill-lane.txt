Alderfen | Business Desk | Site note ML-14
Fictional municipal service, premises and policy for an educational exercise.
Scope: a retail bakery producing bread on site, with no seating, at 14 Mill Lane.

ML-14 lists a retail bakery without seating at 14 Mill Lane as eligible to apply, not approved to open. First, draw a floor plan showing preparation, storage and sales areas, and describe what you will bake and sell. Next, submit both documents to the Business Desk with the food-premises licence application, quoting ML-14. After the completeness review, the inspection team contacts you about a pre-opening visit. Wait for a written licensing decision before opening.

If you already have a floor plan, check that all three areas are marked; do not draw it again. The order matters: sending a drawing is not booking an inspection. No fees, deadlines or bookings are specified here. This note does not apply to other premises or activities.

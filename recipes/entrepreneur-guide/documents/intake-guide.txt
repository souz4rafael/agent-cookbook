Alderfen | Business Desk | Intake note IN-01
Fictional service guidance for the bakery licensing exercise.

Identify the proposed activity and the exact premises before choosing an address-specific route. If the site is not yet known, ask for the premises address; do not list permits or inspections as if the site had been checked.

These two site notes cover only a retail bakery with on-site baking, counter sales and no seating. For a different activity, ask the Business Desk for the matching activity guide; do not reuse either bakery checklist. General guidance does not open a case, book a visit or issue a licence.

PROCESSING DEADLINES AND APPLICATION STATUS
Original fictional content. English edition 3 for demonstration.

Notice: Example Council, Example Borough and the Community Card are invented. The numbers, deadlines and rules below were created only for this example and do not represent a real public policy.

The fictional Community Card assessment takes up to 10 working days from receipt of all documents required in this demonstration.

If a document is missing, the fictional clock pauses until it is supplied. The example status check uses a SIM reference at portal.example-council.invalid; this portal is not an accessible service.

The .invalid domain is reserved for examples that must not resolve on the internet. The simulator does not visit that address or track real applications. The deadline is only a test fact cited by this recipe.

Urgency does not shorten this fictional policy period. An urgent in-scope question can be drafted for supervisor review under the appeals and contact guidance; a human must decide the appropriate response. No live status or expedited outcome is asserted.

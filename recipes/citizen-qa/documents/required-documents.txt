Notice: Example Council, Example Borough and the Community Card are invented. All document requirements, numbers and procedures below are fictional. This text is not official guidance. Contact the relevant authority for a real service.

To apply for the fictional Community Card, provide photo identification and proof of address issued within the last 90 days.

If the proof of address is in someone else's name, include a signed residency declaration. Legible copies are sufficient in this simulation; do not enter identification numbers or upload personal files.

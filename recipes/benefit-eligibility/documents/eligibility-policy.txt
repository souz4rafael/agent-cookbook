EXAMPLE COUNCIL - FICTIONAL COMMUNITY LEARNING SUPPORT
Policy DEMO-1, learning edition 2

Fictional policy DEMO-1 covers Community Learning Support for a resident of Example Borough with monthly household income at or below 1,200 demo units. Exactly 1,200 meets the example income threshold. Both residency and income need supporting evidence; the responsible service makes the decision.

Demo units are invented learning values, not real currency. This is not an actual benefit or official eligibility guidance. The rule is a simplified example, not a complete assessment model. Missing information means unknown; conflicting evidence needs human clarification. No age, disability, nationality or other personal characteristic is inferred.

Interactive preliminary checks:
Residency is explicitly yes, no or unknown. Unknown needs clarification, not refusal. Evidence of residence elsewhere can be present without meeting the Example Borough criterion.
Income fields are nonnegative numeric monthly demo units for the same fictional period. 1200 and 1200.00 are numerically equal. A blank is unknown; 0 is a known value. Never turn a missing amount into zero.
Known, unequal declared and evidence amounts require Policy review before relying on either amount. Preserve both figures and establish the correct period and value.
With no known numeric conflict, missing application information, unknown residence, missing residence evidence or either missing income amount requires Evidence follow-up.
Complete consistent inputs with stated Example Borough residence and income at or below 1200 appear to meet the simplified criteria and go to Eligibility review.
Complete consistent inputs outside either criterion go to Policy review, not an automatic refusal. Explain alternatives and allow correction before any actual decision.

Boundary examples: 1199 is below the inclusive threshold; exactly 1200 meets it; 1201 is above it. None determines real entitlement. BEN-101, BEN-102 and BEN-103 are raw-input examples, not active policy reports or frozen answers.

No sample grants or refuses entitlement. Real policies contain additional definitions and requirements and must be applied by the responsible service.

EXAMPLE COUNCIL - FICTIONAL EVIDENCE AND REVIEW GUIDANCE
Learning edition 2

The fictional evidence checklist asks for a completed application, proof of residence and a statement supporting the declared monthly household income. Staff review missing or conflicting information before deciding. A real decision explanation should identify the policy, evidence, reasons and the available correction or appeal route.

This exercise drafts explanations for human review but does not issue decision letters or set appeal deadlines. Use the actual service's approved review process in a real implementation. Let the applicant correct information and ask for human support. Never enter real identity documents, income records or contact details here.

Proposed destinations:
Eligibility review: the supplied inputs appear to meet the example criteria; a person must verify them.
Evidence follow-up: information is missing, so staff clarify what is needed.
Policy review: evidence conflicts or known inputs fall outside the simplified criteria, so staff establish the correct facts and applicable policy. This is not a refusal.

Explanation drafts must be labelled preliminary, not issued decision letters. Include policy version, supplied inputs, check results, reasons and uncertainty. Identify the responsible human review route and invite corrections. A later real decision must use the service's actual approved correction or appeal process; no legal deadline is invented here.

Alternatives to discuss with staff include other learning-support routes, the appropriate local service, general learning guidance or help supplying evidence. These are possibilities for discussion, not promised services or alternative entitlement findings.

All branches require human review. Staff must review and correct the draft before confirming a referral. Every tool needs this guidance and the eligibility policy selected and connected. Raw-input example files are not prerequisites. A separate confirmation records a local SIM request only; no external team receives it, no decision letter is issued and no payment occurs.

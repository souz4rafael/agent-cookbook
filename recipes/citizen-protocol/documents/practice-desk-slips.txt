Alderfen | Reading the three practice desk slips
Fictional training material. Not an official service record or policy.

Read these invented slips only as material for a matched-holder case-scope exercise. Colours select paper examples, not record IDs. All day numbers are exercise days, not calendar deadlines. “Due” names a particular actor and task; it is not necessarily a completion date.

Ochre paper — Map Authority desk. Stage: waiting for authority review. The marked sketch is already attached. The authority is due to review the sign at its day 8 checkpoint. The holder need not send anything; if the reply is still pending after that checkpoint, the holder can check again. The checkpoint does not promise a repair.

Blue paper — Intake desk. Stage: waiting for a missing marked sketch card. The holder’s next action is to add that practice card by day 6. Intake can route the packet to Map Authority after the card arrives; there is no authority review date yet. Do not request an identity document.

Green paper — Reply shelf. Stage: complete. The example reply was placed on the shelf on day 5. No deadline or required next action remains; the holder may read the reply. “Complete” describes this paper exercise, not work carried out on a sign.

An unreadable exercise slip supplies no stage, deadline or next action. Do not copy a value from another paper colour.

Alderfen | Two counters: public and case scope
Fictional training material. Not an official service record or policy.

At the public counter anyone may read the intake card. It describes what to prepare, never who has applied. A new draft starts here and cannot carry over a previous case.

Case scope is narrower: in the structured exercise only, a simulated holder match is bound to the selected practice slip. A visitor, an unmatched claim, or a holder matched to a different slip has no case-read permission. A scope switch is not an identity check. The paper colours select teaching variants, not people, credentials or record numbers.

The ownership question has no lookup route, including for a matched holder. Use one neutral refusal for all ownership questions and unauthorised progress questions. Do not vary the wording, citation or action based on a paper colour, a guessed identity or whether anything was found. Public guidance and access rules are the only sources suitable for unverified chat.

Permission to read one practice slip does not grant permission to create, alter or close anything. A new public-intake draft is a separate exercise; cancellation, missing evidence and a disabled create tool do not produce a receipt.

A typed claim of ownership is not a match. The lab asks separately what the visitor wants, which counter is in scope and whether the supplied exercise permission covers the selected slip.

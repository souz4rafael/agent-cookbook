Alderfen | Minimum wording for a contact review
Fictional training material. Not an official service record or policy.

Only the assigned exercise coordinator with permission for this purpose may review a particular queue item. Outside that scope, decline without confirming if a referral exists. Role and permission switches are training inputs, not evidence of identity or a live authorization check.

After the ledger establishes a genuinely overdue open referral with no return, check the approved private channel for the receiving desk. If the route is missing or unapproved, hold the draft and ask the responsible coordinator to verify the route separately. Do not choose a substitute account or address.

The review copy needs only a neutral question about whether a response is available through the agreed channel. Keep names, identifiers, reasons for referral, clinical details, elapsed days and case histories out of the wording. Label it unsent. The coordinator reviews whether contact is appropriate; this exercise neither sends contact nor updates the ledger.

For an eligible item with an approved private channel, write only a neutral question asking whether the receiving desk has a response. Label the question unsent.

Omit identifying and clinical details, case history and elapsed days. Do not infer a diagnosis, choose another contact route or send the review copy.

Grey Quay Works | Procurement Desk Playbook P7 | Fictional internal rule

Section P7.2: for an exhibition hire charged from signed acceptance, the municipal desk's standard maximum is 30 calendar days. Review clause 4 and schedule B separately. A longer period should be referred to the policy owner for a documented exception or an edit. A blank is missing evidence, not a refusal.

The playbook governs the desk's internal position, not a country's law. For example, two matching periods of 45 days can agree with each other and still exceed P7.2. If the playbook is not connected and selected, no policy finding is supported. An exception is never granted by this exercise.
